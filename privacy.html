Privacy policy

Fraud Hound · Last updated November 20, 2026

The short version: Fraud Hound has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your files are analyzed on your phone and never uploaded. The only data that leaves your phone is what's needed to handle the optional Pro purchase.

Your files

When you choose a file or paste numbers, Fraud Hound reads them on your phone to find the amounts and analyze them. Files, amounts and results are never uploaded. When you open a file or export a report, the system may keep a temporary copy in the app's private storage on your phone; it is never sent anywhere, and it is removed when you delete the app.

PDF reports

If you export a PDF report, it is created on your phone. You decide where it goes through your phone's share sheet.

Free scans counter

To count the 3 free scans per day, the app stores a small counter on your phone (on iPhone, in the Keychain). It contains no personal information and is never sent anywhere.

Purchases

Fraud Hound Pro is an optional one-time purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Fraud Hound never sees your payment details.

To check whether you own Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID created by the app, your purchase history for Fraud Hound, and basic technical information such as your device type, system version, app version, country and IP address. This information is not linked to your name or email, and is not used for advertising or tracking. You can read RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

Fraud Hound does not ask for personal information and does not show ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
