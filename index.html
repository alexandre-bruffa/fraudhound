Frequently asked questions

Which files can I scan?

CSV, Excel (XLSX, XLS) and PDFs with selectable text. You can also paste a column of numbers. Bank and card statements, invoice and payment lists, ledgers, expense reports and sales records work best. Scanned PDFs and photos aren't supported yet.

Why does it say my file has too few amounts?

The check needs at least 100 amounts, and 500 or more gives the most reliable result. The amounts should also vary widely, for example from $3 to $30,000. A single balance sheet, salaries, fixed prices or ID numbers aren't a good fit. A file that fails these checks doesn't use a free scan.

My file shows "Irregular". Does that mean fraud?

No. Fraud Hound spots unusual patterns. An unusual result isn't proof of fraud. It's a signal to look closer. Normal business reasons, such as fixed prices, approval limits or a small file, can also change the pattern.

What do Depth 1, 2 and 3 mean?

Depth 1 compares how often amounts start with 1, 2, 3… 9 with real-world financial data. Depth 2 looks at the first two figures and the second figure, which catches amounts kept just under a limit. Depth 3 checks endings, repeated and round amounts, and lists the rows worth checking. Depth 2 and 3 are part of Fraud Hound Pro.

I bought Pro but features are still locked

In the app, open Settings and tap "Restore purchase". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy Pro.

Do I need to pay again on a new phone?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase. On a new phone with the same Apple ID or Google account, tap "Restore purchase" in Settings.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Files are read and analyzed on your phone and never leave it. There's no account and no server.
